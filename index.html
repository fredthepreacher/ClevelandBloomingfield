Medicare Plan Options

A

Medicare Part A

Hospital insurance — inpatient care, skilled nursing, hospice

B

Medicare Part B

Medical insurance — doctor visits, outpatient services, preventive care

C

Medicare Advantage (Part C)

All-in-one alternative to Original Medicare — often includes dental, vision & hearing

D

Part D Prescription Drug

Covers prescription medications — choose the plan that covers your specific drugs

SUP

Medicare Supplement (Medigap)

Fills the gaps Original Medicare doesn't cover — predictable out-of-pocket costs

Annuities — Guaranteed Income You Can't Outlive

In an uncertain market, annuities offer a safe harbor. I help you understand your options and build a retirement strategy that generates reliable income for life.

Fixed Annuities

Guaranteed interest rate with no market risk. Your principal is protected and grows at a locked-in rate — ideal for conservative savers who want predictability.

Fixed Indexed Annuities

Growth linked to market indexes like the S&P 500 — with a floor that protects you from losses. Participate in market upside without downside risk.
